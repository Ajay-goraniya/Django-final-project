WHAT I TRIED AND WILL NOT SHIP (tested today, both weeks).
The recalibrated model (R-26 arm b, identical coefficients, 152-knot isotonic) looked like the one real win:
on week 2 it is better than frozen at every slippage level, 8/9 positive days against 7/9 falling to 6/9.
I built it as a drop-in - learner/v12_2/model_v10_recal.json, one field group changed from the shipped artifact -
and then ran it on WEEK 1, where it LOSES to frozen at every level: +135.79 against +142.93 at 0 cents,
7/8 positive days against 8/8, on the identical 475 fires. Better one week, worse the other, same direction call.
That is the rain-or-sun test and it fails it. Not shipping. The file stays in the tree as a tested artifact, unused.

THE ONE DEFECT WORTH FIXING, and it is not in the model.
124 of 209 live orders - 59% - die on a single venue error, "no orders found to match". That is a marketable
order arriving at a price the resting book no longer has. It is one cause, not variance, and it is the whole
difference between filling 23% of candles and filling the 35% we already try for. The trades we win are already
priced right; we simply do not get most of them. Second, 1,228 decisions were blocked by "Waiting for fresh UP and
DOWN books" - Mumbai's Task 111 measured that same bar refusing 24.7% of seconds where the venue WAS quoting both
sides, purely because no delta had arrived for 5 s.
Neither is a signal problem, and no amount of retraining touches either.

CORRECTION TO THE LINE ABOVE, made before acting on it. "59% of orders die on one venue error" counts ATTEMPTS,
not candles, and it is misleading. The retry apparatus works: the attempt histogram is {1: 164, 2: 37, 3: 8}, and
of the 57 candles that needed more than one attempt, 44 went on to fill. **At the candle level we fill 84 of 129
attempted = 65%,** not 40%. I had the denominator wrong.

WHAT THE 45 UNFILLED CANDLES ACTUALLY ARE, checked in the journal rather than assumed. 42 of 45 stop at attempt 1
with a median 1,977 ms of budget still unspent - so they did NOT run out of time. The retry fires, re-reads the
book, and the re-plan then refuses: `order_plan` raises on the moved price and the lane releases rather than
chasing. That is the EV guard doing its job. Paying up to follow a book that has moved away is exactly the trade
the guard exists to decline, and "fix the rejections" would mean buying worse prices on purpose.
**So there is no execution bug here to fix. I proposed one on a bad denominator.**

WHERE THE ACTUAL GAP IS, and it is upstream of the order. The model calls 54% of candles; we place an order on
35%. The missing ~19 points are decisions that never reach the executor at all, and one reason dominates:
**1,228 decide rows blocked by "Waiting for fresh UP and DOWN books"** against 23 blocked by EV and 67 by warm-up.
Mumbai's Task 111 measured that same bar from the other side: of the seconds it refuses, the venue was quoting
BOTH sides on 87.3%, and the refusals are entirely the age test - 244 of 987 both-sided seconds (24.7%) blocked
solely because no delta had arrived for 5 s or more, with zero attributable to one-sided books or delta handling.

THAT is the candidate, and it is a THRESHOLD, so it does not move on an argument. It moves on a grid against real
outcomes, both arms, every cell, or not at all. The data needed is per-second book age joined to what the trade
would have settled at - Mumbai has the raw stream and the outcome table; the feature parquets carry no age column,
so this cannot be gridded from here.
