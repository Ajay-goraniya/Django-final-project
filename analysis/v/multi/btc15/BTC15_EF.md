# BTC 15-minute Polymarket up/down on its own (V/multi/btc15, 09-27)

**Verdict (14 days 09-13..09-26, 1,343 candles, walk-forward by day, 12 test days, graded on gamma's own resolution):**
- **EF does NOT work on BTC 15m alone:** 0 of 12 cells is positive under London execution (best -0.142/$1). Venue-only (i) loses in every cell (paper -0.25..-0.90, 10/12 days negative).
- Model-only (iii) paper is +0.02..+0.12 at theta >= 0.10, but permutation p is 0.03-0.20 and 5-6/12 days are negative. verify: NOT A FINDING (fails permutation, sweep and cost; gone at +2c).
- **The lead is smaller and lasts only a few seconds longer, not minutes.** After a model-only fire the venue moves our way ~+2.4c on 15m vs ~+3.7c on BTC 5m (same days, same code). In the first second: +0.8..1.2c vs +1.7..2.4c.
- Priced 1 s late, paper is -0.01..+0.09 on 15m (SE ~0.07) vs -0.05..0.00 on 5m. Priced 5 s late: 15m -0.05..+0.05, 5m -0.05..-0.12. There is no tradeable window beyond ~2-3 s.
- **Data:** 705 prints per candle (BTC 5m: 1,409). Ask-proxy age p50 4 s / p90 18 s (5m: 2 s / 3 s). An exec print exists within 4 s only 65% of the time. Spread p50 1.0c from prints; live book (one 16-min sample, 09-27 03:38-03:54): spread 1c, $42 at best ask p50 (5m: 1c, $71).
- **Settlement:** the 15m event text is the same as the 5m text (Chainlink BTC/USD TWAP-60s, closing TWAP >= opening). Line = TWAP60 before the open. Binance TWAP60 direction agrees with gamma on **98.5%** of 1,343 candles (5m: 97.8%).
- **Comparator warning:** BTC 5m (ii) venue+move shows +0.12/+0.25 London-exec at theta 0.15/0.25. It is a same-second latency race: the fired side moves +7c within 1 s, and paper goes +0.64 -> +0.15 (1 s) -> -0.01 (2 s). **Not a finding.**

## Setup (defined before looking; reuses ../fetch_poly.py, ../build_panel.py, ../analyze.py)
- Market: gamma slug `btc-updown-15m-{epoch}` (series `btc-up-or-down-15m`), epoch = candle open, multiple of 900.
  Resolution text (btc-updown-15m-1790477100): "resolve to Up if the TWAP of Bitcoin, generated by Chainlink, of the time range
  ... is >= the price at the beginning of that range ... BTC/USD TWAP data stream (btc-usd-twap-60s-streams)". This is identical to the 5m text.
- How ../build_panel.py does it, and how it is reused here: `line` = mean of Binance 1 s closes over [ep-60, ep-1]. Settle proxy = the same mean over the last 60 s.
  The move = ln(P/line) / (sig1s * sqrt(time to the closing window + 20)). The 5m window ends at 240 s, so it never enters the last minute.
  build_panel15.py keeps this exactly and adds the projected closing TWAP60 for s > CL-60 (known closes + current price for the rest).
  The 15m decision grid ends at 840 s = CL-60, where the projection equals the current price, so it has no effect on this grid.
- Labels = gamma outcomePrices (the venue's own resolution). Cross-checks: post-close prints 9/9 (thin); Binance TWAP60 98.5%.
- Data: gamma (1,344 markets) and data-api /trades (1.09 M taker prints, none hit the 10k offset cap). Binance 1 s BTCUSDT klines come from data-api.binance.vision.
- Ask proxy: a taker BUY of a side at p is that side's ask; a taker SELL of the other side at q is an ask of 1-q. Per-second mean.
  **Quote-age rule (as in ETH_SOL_EF.md):** decide on a past print <= 5 s old. The price paid is the FIRST print at-or-after the decision second (<= 4 s later). verify.quote_age passes.
- Arms: (i) Platt on [logit mid, logit mid * s/840]; (ii) (i) + [zt, zt * s/840]; (iii) [zt, zt * s/840], no venue.
  Decision/training grid s = 15, 30, ..., 840 (CL-60). One trade per candle: the first grid second with max-side EV >= theta, ask 0.02..0.98.
- Scoring as ETH_SOL_EF.md: London-exec fill 54.1% if it would win / 65.0% if it would lose. Slippage p10/p50/p90 -1/+2/+11c goes on top of the exec print. Fee 0.07*sh*p*(1-p), $10, 1,000 runs.
  Permutation = shuffle the sides, and price each flipped side at ITS OWN exec print. Paired = McNemar vs (i).
- T5 lead lifetime (new): each cell's fires are re-priced k s after the decision. Price = the first print in [s+k, s+k+4], else the latest print since s, so every fire is kept.
  "drift" = the fired side's price change in cents, i.e. how far the venue moves toward the model after we decide.
- Comparator: the same scripts on BTC 5m (CL=300, grid 15..240), same 14 days (cached ../ data). The 5m grid is every 15 s, so its arm-(i) fire counts differ from ETH_SOL_EF.md (every 1 s).
- Depth at best: data-api trades carry no book. Print sizes are a lower bound (median taker BUY $5.0, p90 $36.6). One live public-book sample: book_snap.py (09-27 03:38-03:54 UTC, every 10 s; s 15..CL-60, ask 0.05..0.95): 15m 84 snapshots, spread p25/p50/p75 1.0/1.0/1.0c, $ at best ask p25/p50/p75 14/42/94; 5m 68 snapshots, spread 1.0/1.0/1.0c, $ at best ask 21/71/212. One quarter-hour only: indicative.
