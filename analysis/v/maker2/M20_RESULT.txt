5494 candles, two independent periods: P1 09-11..22 (3456), P2 09-23..10-01 (2038). Fill = print strictly below our bid.

ANSWER (sim): NONE of the probe's ingredients makes money on history. The probe rule itself
(mkt join-bid, band 0.60-0.80, calm, sec 60-180, 2 bps cancel) = -0.028/$ (P1, 1648 fills) and -0.009/$ (P2, 1018).
All 16 mkt cells lose in BOTH periods (-0.009..-0.044/$), both halves negative in every one.
The 2 bps cancel changes nothing: BTC almost never moves 2 bps in 1 s in calm candles. Even a PERFECT
same-second cancel (optimistic bound) at 1 or 2 bps: -0.026..-0.028 (P1), -0.009 (P2).
This agrees with STRICT_PFAV_CALM.txt (09-30): same rule, 20 days, -335..-778, positive only 09-27..30.

So the live +45 (94 fills, ~80% win) is NOT explained by the rule. Either (a) the period - the rule's
historical good stretch is exactly when the probe went live - or (b) real fills at the touch are less adverse
than "print through bid" (live win ~80% vs 66-67% in sim). Zurich's live breakdown (WHY_MAKER_WINS.txt) tests (b).
Until (b) is shown, treat the live result as a good stretch, not an edge. Do not scale.

M19 side-note: its sim edge sits OUTSIDE the probe's filters - window 60-180 alone turns +0.084/+0.059 into
-0.007/-0.015, band 0.60-0.80 alone into -0.048/-0.049. M19's sim money is in cheap sides and early/late
seconds, exactly where the live paper shows adverse fills.
