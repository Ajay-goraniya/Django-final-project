# Nine-way line-by-line audit, 09-16. Live-safety items first.

Nine auditors, one per segment of the engine. Two defects were fixed in 12.15.0 (6ceb609) after V verified each
one against the running artifact rather than the report. The rest are recorded here; nothing else has been changed.

## VERIFIED AND FIXED (12.15.0)
1. **The venue fee was recorded as zero on every live fill.** Local `sum(pnl)` +4.4866 against the venue's
   `sum(venue_pnl)` −8.5073; difference 12.9939 = `sum(venue_fees)` 12.9947. The reported profit was uncharged
   fees and the true sign is negative. `payout − spent − venue_fees == venue_pnl` on 83/83 rows.
2. **REVERSAL was handed the probability of the side it was not buying.** DOWN reversal with `fair_p_up` 0.0100
   arrived at the EV gate as p=0.0100 against P(DOWN)=0.9900. DOWN reversals could never clear EV.

## LIVE-SAFETY, VERIFIED BY V, NOT YET CHANGED — these are the owner's calls
3. **Live stake mode is `streak`, which is equity-scaled.** Read from the live journal meta:
   `{'mode':'streak','percent':5.0,'win_trigger':3.0,'loss_trigger':2.0,'min_stake':3.0,'max_stake':50.0}`.
   `poly_dashboard.py:141-142` runs the SAME assignment after `win_trigger` wins OR `loss_trigger` losses:
   `current = clamp(running_equity * percent/100)`. At today's ~$57 equity that yields 3.00, which the
   `min_stake` floor pins to the current stake — so **nothing is escalating right now**. But it scales with the
   bankroll: $100 → 5.00, $200 → 10.00, $400 → 20.00, $1000 → 50.00. The owner's rule is "stake stays fixed;
   Kelly/dynamic never goes live without TWO separate confirmations", and `apply()` requires exactly one
   (`poly_dashboard.py:256`). `DEFAULT_STAKE` is `mode='ladder'` (`:9`), also equity-scaled, and `:156` exempts
   ladder from the operator's own `max_stake` bound. **Stake is the owner's and V has changed nothing.**
4. **The one owner-ordered stop can be skipped silently.** `_floor_check` sits inside one try block that begins
   with up to four 8 s `metadata()` awaits; any timeout jumps to `except` and the floor is simply not evaluated
   that cycle, with only an error string as evidence — while `venue_truth_loop` keeps `cash` fresh so trading
   continues. Separately the floor's `open_value` is read `ORDER BY ts DESC LIMIT 1` with **no age bound**, so if
   venue-truth is failing while positions settle into cash, equity is overstated and the floor never fires.
5. **A fired floor is invisible on the dashboard.** The page renders `controls.master_enabled` and `live.halt`
   only; `ef_enabled=False` — including after `_floor_check` acts — still displays as "LIVE · MASTER ON".
   `ef_cash_floor` is displayed by nothing and settable by nothing; it can only be armed by editing meta.
6. **`gather()` has no `return_exceptions`**, and `reconcile_loop`, `grade_loop`, `chart_seed` and part of
   `venue()` are unguarded. One raise unwinds `main()`; systemd restarts; safe-start parks master OFF. That is
   the 09-16 four-hour silent disarm, reachable from a single exception.
7. **Paper is not a proxy for live.** Paper fills 100% of its orders where live fills 65% of candles, and charges
   a 7% fee against the venue's 0-bps tape. Both errors are large and they do not cancel. Any comparison of a
   paper per-$1 against a live per-$1 compared a 100%-fill strategy to a 65%-fill one.
