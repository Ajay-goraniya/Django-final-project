a different row subset, not a different model.
**Arm (b) does NOT reproduce.** H1 reports (b) +182.17 on 778 fires; on the pushed `model_b_week1.json` through
the same harness that reproduces frozen to the cent, I get **+145.45 on 733 fires** - a gap of $36.72 against
frozen of essentially zero, not +$36. Since frozen agrees bit-for-bit, the difference is in the EVALUATION of (b),
not in the artifact. Raised with H1. **Until it is reconciled, "an arbitrary calibrator detail is worth $36" is
NOT an established finding and must not be relayed as one.**
