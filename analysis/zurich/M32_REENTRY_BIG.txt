THE ZERO-FEE COLUMN IS AN UPPER BOUND ON AN UPPER BOUND AND MUST NOT BE READ AS ACHIEVABLE:
 1. a maker order earns its zero fee by POSTING AND WAITING. This simulation takes the touch the
    instant the trigger prints - arm/sell at the bid, re-buy at the ask - which is a TAKER action.
    Zero fee AND an immediate fill at the touch is the best of both worlds and cannot be had at once.
 2. these entries are taker entries BY DEFINITION: FAV and EF both buy the ask. A zero-fee entry
    for them is counterfactual, not a cheaper version of the same trade.
 3. M30's maker fidelity gate failed on this box at 75.4% against an 85% bar, so what a resting
    order would really have filled at cannot be simulated here at all.
To test the owner's rule as actual maker orders, the exits and re-entries would have to be POSTED and
their fills measured live - which is the same conclusion M30 reached about the maker rule itself.
