2. WHY I AM NOT CROWNING PFAV_taker, AND WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS
   PFAV_taker was never a strategy. pfav.py:129-139 built it as a PAIRING REFERENCE: it takes the
   FIRST taker BUY print on the tape inside sec 60-180 whose price is in 0.60-0.80, on whichever
   token that print happened to be on, and grades it against the winner. Its "entry price" is the
   price somebody else got. Three sibling arms on the same candles with the same window and band
   (PFAV, PFAV50, PFAV_THRU) are all at -0.053 to -0.057. An arm that is the mirror image of its
   own family with t +8.95 is the shape this project has repeatedly found to be an artefact, so I
   checked it before reporting it rather than after.

3. WHAT THE CHECKS FOUND - IT DID NOT DISSOLVE, AND HERE IS THE DECOMPOSITION
   a) PRICE IS REACHABLE. The arm books 0.6898; the own-side ASK at that same second is 0.6897,
      at +1s 0.6959, +2s 0.7013, +10s 0.7085. The price is not a phantom.
   b) TIMING IS NOT THE EXPLANATION. PFAV_taker and PFAV share the window (60-180) and band
      (0.60-0.80) and their median entry seconds are 84.5 and 83.0. The 14-point spread between
      them is price and selection, not information arriving later. (PFAV's own price is the path
      MAXIMUM bid in the band, pfav.py:100 - which makes PFAV look worse than a real resting
      strategy would be. Worth knowing separately.)
   c) MY FIRST CONTROL WAS CONTAMINATED AND I AM REPORTING THAT. Restricting "buy the favourite at
      the first in-band ask" to the candles PFAV_taker fired on gave +5.38pp, which contradicted
      M44/M52. It contradicted them because the subset itself was selected on the print existing.
   d) THE CLEAN CONTROL REPRODUCES M44/M52 EXACTLY. Same rule on the FULL universe since PFAV_taker
      started - 1,454 candles with a book and a label, no print condition anywhere:
        gap -0.42pp, per $1 -0.0061, t -0.35.  Fairly priced, as six prior studies found.
      And the print condition SPLITS that fairly-priced universe almost symmetrically:
        candles WITH an in-band taker print (n 992):  +5.38pp   per $1 +0.0770  t +3.89
        candles WITHOUT one          (n 462):        -12.90pp   per $1 -0.1845  t -5.48
      A fairly-priced whole that splits +5.4 / -12.9 on an observable condition is information, not
      noise. The favourite nobody lifts is badly overpriced; the favourite somebody lifts is cheap.
   e) THE EXECUTABLE FORM SURVIVES REALISTIC LATENCY. Act ON the print, pay the ask d seconds later,
      taker fee included:
        +0s +11.22pp per $1 +0.1751 t +9.14 | +1s +10.62 +0.1558 | +2s +10.10 +0.1412
        +3s  +9.99pp per $1 +0.1376 t +7.79 | +5s  +9.68 +0.1297 | +10s +9.39 +0.1191
      The documented data-api trade lag is 2.2 s, so +3s is the realistic row: +0.1376 per $1,
      t +7.79, halves +0.1070/+0.1681, 7/7 green days, every single day positive INCLUDING 10-04,
      the day FAV lost 149.69.
   f) THE FLAG IS THE VENUE'S OWN. is_taker comes from Polymarket's takerOnly=true subset and side
      comes straight from the API (tape_rec.py:41-49, 75), so the signal does not rest on any
      classification of mine. Maker is the derived side, and this finding does not use it.

4. WHY THIS IS COHERENT RATHER THAN SUSPICIOUS
   It is the mirror image of the one result that has run through this entire project. Every passive
   test here has lost to adverse selection - our resting bid fills precisely when the side is going
   wrong (PFAV -2.86pp; ask-touch fill rates 41-46%; M43; the London maker). If the taker who lifts
   the favourite is the informed one, then FOLLOWING that taker must win by the same amount that
   RESTING against it loses. PFAV at -2.86pp and PFAV_taker at +11.22pp are one fact seen from both
   sides, measured on identical candles. That is why I am reporting it instead of discarding it.

5. WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE BEFORE ANYONE TRADES THIS - NOT YET ESTABLISHED
   i.   It has NEVER been run forward. Everything above is a reconstruction from the recorded tape.
   ii.  DEPTH IS UNTESTED. I assumed we can buy at the recorded ask. On FAV, 16% of decisions miss on
        price and 5-6% on size, and depth truncation is why the partial model exists. This is the
        single most likely way the +9.99pp shrinks, and it is exactly where every previous
        promising number on this box has died.
   iii. DETECTION LATENCY IS UNMEASURED. I show the gap survives to +10s, but nobody has measured
        how fast this box can see a data-api print and act on it. The 2.2 s figure is the feed's
        documented lag, not a measured round trip for this path.
   iv.  n 994 over SEVEN days, one market, one venue, no out-of-sample split. 7/7 green is 7 days.
   My recommendation is a prereg and a paper arm, not a stake: register the rule (first in-band
   taker BUY print, sec 60-180, band 0.60-0.80, buy that side at the then-ask, $10, taker fee),
   run it forward with the real feed and a real depth check, and let it earn its own n. I am not
   proposing any change to anything live, and I have not touched a single running process.
