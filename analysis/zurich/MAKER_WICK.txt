VERDICT (Zurich, 10-02 00:0x). The "wick" hypothesis is NOT supported by these 33 fills.
1. A WICK WOULD SHOW UP BEFORE THE FILL. It does not. Bucketed on bn_before, the pre-fill move
   against us, the big bucket (<0.5 bps, n=25) is 16W/9L at -0.1013/$1 and the one fill in the
   1.5-2.0 bucket WON. There is no gradient: the fills that lost were not the ones taken into a
   move. Whatever is wrong is not visible in the second before the fill, which is the only part of
   this that a rule could ever act on.
2. WHAT SEPARATES WINNERS FROM LOSERS IS A SUSTAINED POST-FILL MOVE, NOT A SPIKE. Max adverse move
   from fill to candle end: <5 bps 20W/5L +0.1393/$1; 5-10 bps 0W/6L -1.0000/$1; 10-20 bps 0W/2L
   -1.0000/$1; >=20 bps empty. Every single fill that saw >=5 bps against it lost, all 8 of them.
   READ THIS WITH CARE: max adverse move after the fill is close to a restatement of the outcome -
   if the price finishes against you, you lost - and it is unknowable at post time. It is a
   description, not a gate, and nobody should build one on it.
3. A WICK IMPLIES REVERSION. These did not revert. Our token's own prints after the fill bottom at
   0.00-0.01 on 13 of 13 losers, first crossing under 0.50 at a median ~sec 170, and they stayed
   there to settlement. That is a market repricing, not a spike.
4. THE LAST MINUTE DID NOT TAKE THE LOSSES. 12 of 13 losers were ALREADY behind at sec 240; exactly
   ONE (id 70, 23:25 UP @0.60) was ahead at 240 and lost in the final 60 s. The TWAP60 proxy used
   for that column agrees with the venue's own resolution on 33 of 33 candles (100%).
5. AGAINST THE HYPOTHESIS, the clearest counter-examples are the two losers whose Binance move
   barely happened at all: id 50 (21:00 UP @0.61, max adverse 1.6 bps) and id 67 (23:10 UP @0.63,
   1.7 bps). Both finished on the wrong side of the TWAP line having never been wicked.
