STEP 1 (B) VERDICT, AND IT CHANGES HOW (A) SHOULD BE READ.

FIRST, A CORRECTION TO MY OWN CLAIM THAT GOT (B) APPROVED. I told V the dumps would carry LESS
reconstruction error than the tape1s harness. Half right, and the half I missed cancels the gain:
individual trades do remove the per-second aggregation error, but no Chainlink sample exists for
these days, so on_ref_price is never fed, ref_src is 0.0 and EVERY open-relative feature measures
from the Binance spot TWAP60 proxy instead of the settlement line the engine had live. The depth
test never covered that, so it was not inside the 0.013-0.015 I quoted. Measured against the
engine's own journalled p on the 09-15..09-16 overlap: mean|dp| 0.0245 on 5,147 comparable passes,
within 0.02 on 58%, and the reconstruction picks the OPPOSITE SIDE on a further 235 passes (4.4%).
That is the same error as the tape1s harness (0.022-0.034), not better. (B) is therefore a WEAKER
test than (A), which used the journalled rows and carried none of this. Reported as such.

SECOND, (B) ON ITS OWN POINTS THE OTHER WAY FROM (A) - FOR ONE ARM, BY A HAIR.
  FIXED/taker  13-24 +0.0158 (n 117)   00-13 -0.0326 (n 76)
On the arithmetic that is the prereg's prediction satisfied: 13-24 > 00-13 and 13-24 > 0. It fails
on RAW/taker (13-24 -0.0621, worse than 00-13's -0.0500 and negative). I am not going to call that
a pass. +0.0158 per $1 is 1.6 cents on the dollar from a harness whose p is wrong by 0.0245 and
whose side flips on 4.4% of passes - the claimed edge is smaller than the measurement error that
produced it.

THIRD, AND THIS IS THE REAL RESULT: PUT THE SE ON IT AND THE SESSION EFFECT IS NOT THERE AT ALL.
The 13-24 minus 00-13 difference, with its own standard error, on 15 days and every arm x execution:
  COMBINED FIXED/taker      -0.0470 +/- 0.1087   t -0.43
  COMBINED RAW/taker        -0.1228 +/- 0.1031   t -1.19
  COMBINED FIXED/ask-touch  +0.0709 +/- 0.1551   t +0.46
  COMBINED RAW/ask-touch    -0.0893 +/- 0.1400   t -0.64

Not one difference clears |t| 1.96. Every taker cell is also indistinguishable from zero in its own
right (|t| <= 1.63 everywhere, combined FIXED/taker -0.0137 +/- 0.0531). So the honest reading is
not that the session effect inverts - it is that THERE IS NO MEASURABLE SESSION EFFECT IN EITHER
DIRECTION, and the taker rule itself is a coin flip after fees across 363 FIXED fires on 15 days.
THIS TEMPERS MY OWN (A) WRITE-UP. (A)'s inversion (13-24 -0.0947 vs 00-13 +0.0598) is arithmetically
exactly what I reported, and it does kill the prediction - a finding that needed 13-24 > 0 got a
negative number on unseen days. But I leaned on the INVERSION as though the reversed sign were
itself the result, and at t -0.43 combined it is not. The prediction fails; the opposite claim is
not thereby established. London's 13-24 +151.26 / all-hours -84.10 was nine days of noise, and so
is any session verdict drawn from 15 days at this fire count.
