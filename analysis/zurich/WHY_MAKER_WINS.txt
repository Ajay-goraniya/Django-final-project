VERDICT (Zurich, 10-02 09:3x). WHICH INGREDIENT CARRIES THE PROBE?
THE PRICE BAND, and only the price band - and it is n=5, so this is a direction, not a result.
 1. BAND (a) is the one factor that separates in the probe's direction on M19's OWN data, and it
    does so in BOTH margins: in-band 0.60-0.80 gives +0.2012/$1 (m0.15, n 5) and +0.2308/$1
    (m0.20, n 5), against out-of-band -0.1160 (n 89) and -0.1356 (n 73). The probe, which is
    in-band by construction on all 96 fills, sits at +0.1314/$1. Same sign, same rough size, two
    independent margins. Five fills each, so INSUFFICIENT - but it is the only factor that points
    the right way twice.
 2. THE CALM GATE POINTS THE WRONG WAY. On M19, calm candles are WORSE: -0.3976/$1 (m0.15, n 19)
    and -0.2401/$1 (m0.20, n 16) versus +0.0296 and -0.0349 for non-calm. If calm were the probe's
    engine, M19's calm subset should be its best cell and it is its worst. So the probe's edge is
    NOT the calm gate, on this evidence. The probe cannot test this itself: vol < 0.304 is true on
    96 of 96 of its fills, by construction, so it has no contrast to offer.
 3. THE 60-180 s WINDOW IS INCONSISTENT. m0.15 inside the window +0.0959 vs outside -0.1772, which
    helps; m0.20 inside -0.0998 vs outside -0.0756, which hurts. A factor that flips sign between
    two margins on the same candles is noise.
 4. THE 2 bps ADVERSE TEST CANNOT BE READ. Only 3 probe fills and 1 M19 fill ever had >= 2 bps
    against them in the second before the fill. There is nothing to measure yet; that is what the
    rule is FOR, and it means the rule is rarely binding rather than that it is working.
 5. ON THE NINE CANDLE-SIDES BOTH SYSTEMS TOOK, THE PROBE DID WORSE: -0.2634/$1 against M19's
    -0.1736/$1, because the probe paid 0.60-0.61 where M19 paid 0.28-0.67. Five unique candle-sides.
    This is the single most uncomfortable row in the file and it belongs here: where they met head
    to head, paying the favourite's price was the more expensive choice.
 6. SIZE AND WINDOW CAVEAT, which limits all of the above. The probe's +45 lifetime is concentrated:
    the 09-30 17:00 candle alone is +24.05 of it, and its own second half by time is NEGATIVE
    (MAKER_60.txt). Restricted to M19's window the probe is +5.7308 on 21 fills (+0.0859/$1) - still
    positive, but small, and the head-to-head subset inside that window is negative. The probe has
    never traded a single fill outside its own gates, so nothing here can separate "the band works"
    from "the probe has been lucky in-band for two days".
