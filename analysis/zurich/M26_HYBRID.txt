TEST A VERDICT IN WORDS (Zurich, 10-02 13:2x). HYBRID FAILS, and it fails on the exact risk the
prereg named: the fallback is too late.
 - Hybrid -0.0072/$1 (-7.55) against FAV taker +0.0211/$1 (+32.44) and probe-only +0.1271/$1
   (+35.96). Hybrid is WORSE THAN EITHER COMPONENT, which is the clearest possible verdict.
 - The deciding number: the fallback leg on its own is 79 trades, 57W/22L, -43.51 on $770.28 =
   -0.0565 per $1. It does not merely add less than hoped; it loses.
 - WHY, in one comparison. The fallback enters at mean sec 128 at an ask of 0.7643. FAV, on those
   SAME candles, enters at mean sec 71 at 0.7212 and makes +0.0696/$1. So waiting for the maker
   window to expire costs 4.3 cents on the ask and turns +0.0696 into -0.0565. The hybrid's phase 2
   is not "FAV as a fallback", it is "FAV 57 seconds late and 4 cents worse", and that is the whole
   difference. This is the same fact M25 found from the other direction: in calm-FAV, WHEN you buy
   dominates, and the price the clock charges is larger than the edge.
 - Halves: +17.02 then -24.56, so it also fails the both-halves test.
 - Per day it is not uniform: hybrid beats FAV badly on 10-02 (-0.28 vs -64.97) because the probe's
   real fills carried the day and only 4 fallbacks fired, and loses badly on 10-01 (-50.77 vs +44.99)
   where 51 fallbacks fired at -67.25. Hybrid cushions bad days and spoils good ones.
 - One thing worth keeping: probe-only is still the best per $1 by a wide margin (+0.1271 on $283
   deployed), on 76 trades. The hybrid dilutes it by adding a leg with a negative expectation.
WHAT THIS IMPLIES, offered as the next hypothesis and NOT as a proposal: if FAV's edge lives in the
EARLY entry, a fallback must not wait for the maker leg to expire - it would have to take at FAV's
own first qualifying second while the bid still rests, which means two positions in one candle, more
capital per candle, and its own prereg. I am not testing that without V registering it first.

TEST B - WHAT IT WOULD NEED, and why I have not built it.
It is technically straightforward and needs nothing from the live probe or 8787's live path:
  - a standalone paper process with its OWN sqlite (not the shared `fires` table, which pfav.py also
    writes - a new arm there is an avoidable risk to a shared research table), on cron under flock
    like grade.py and m21_watch.py;
  - read-only on maker_probe.sqlite3 for phase 1 (the probe's real fills, which is what makes this a
    hybrid rather than a simulation) and on the shadow's book/vol inputs for phase 2;
  - roughly 80 lines, reusing S.build_rows / S._fav_fill / S._fav_pnl rather than copying them;
  - no change to the probe, no restart of anything, no credentials.
I have NOT built it, because it would spend four days of forward candles measuring a rule that just
failed its own retro on 202 calm candles with the fallback leg negative at -0.0565/$1. If V wants it
forward anyway - the retro window is short and 10-01 dominates the loss - say so and it is a short
job. The variant I would rather spend the forward capacity on is the concurrent-fallback version
above, once it is registered.
