Polymarket has no buy-stop - a resting BUY at 0.60 with the ask near 0.50 fills at once -
so the rule is: first time a side's ask reaches 0.60, buy it as a taker. Both sides can
trigger. TRAIL = stop at peak_bid - 0.10; OWN = the owner's % ratchet. R0 no re-buy, R1 re-buy
at ask >= 0.60. Stops sell at the triggering bid. Held at 295 settles on venues.outcome/gamma.

M44 VERDICT: EVERY CELL LOSES WITH FEES, AND THE ZERO-FEE COLUMN SAYS WHY - THE 0.60 TRIGGER HAS
NO EDGE AT ALL, SO THERE IS NOTHING FOR A STOP TO PROTECT.

WITH THE REAL TAKER FEE, which is the only column that describes a trade you could place:
  TRAIL R0  -0.0448 per candle  t -10.50  green 1/12   buys/candle 1.56
  OWN   R0  -0.0467             t -10.27  green 1/12   buys/candle 1.56
  TRAIL R1  -0.1956             t -17.67  green 0/12   buys/candle 5.98
  OWN   R1  -0.1828             t -16.41  green 0/12   buys/candle 5.49
No session is positive in any of them (best is EU at -0.036, worst US at -0.053 on TRAIL R0).

THE ZERO-FEE COLUMN IS THE DIAGNOSIS: TRAIL R0 is +0.0022 per candle at t +0.54 and OWN R0 is
-0.0000 at t -0.00. That is break-even to four decimal places on 2,547 candles. Buying a 5-minute
binary at 0.60 the moment it reaches 0.60 is a FAIRLY PRICED bet - the market's 0.60 is right - so
the entry contributes nothing, positive or negative, and the entire loss in the fee column is the
fee. One entry at 0.60 costs 0.07*0.6*0.4 = 0.0168 in fee, and at 1.56 buys a candle plus the exit
fee that is about 0.047 - which is exactly the gap between +0.0022 and -0.0448.
So the answer to the owner is not "your stop is wrong". It is that there is no edge at the entry for
any exit rule to work on: a stop can only protect profit that the entry generates, and at 0.60 the
entry generates none.

RE-BUYING IS THE ONE THING THAT IS ACTIVELY WRONG, AND IT IS NOT JUST THE FEE. R1 is negative even
at ZERO fee - TRAIL R1 -0.0269 at t -3.04, OWN R1 -0.0242 at t -2.70 - so re-entering at 0.60 after
being stopped out is a losing decision in its own right, before any cost. It also quadruples the
turnover (1.56 to 5.98 buys a candle), which is why it is four times worse once fees are charged.
That is the same conclusion M32, M36 and M42 reached by three other routes: re-entry is where this
family of ideas loses its money.
HIS % RATCHET IS NOT THE PROBLEM EITHER, and he should hear that: OWN tracks TRAIL almost exactly
(-0.0467 vs -0.0448 with fees, -0.0000 vs +0.0022 without). His ratchet is neither better nor worse
than the points trail - both are managing a position that has no expected profit to manage.
