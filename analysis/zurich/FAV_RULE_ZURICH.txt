HEADLINE
  LOW-vol REPLICATES, and more cleanly than on V's days: positive in 9 of 9 window x band cells
  (V: 8 of 9), and per $1 the low bucket beats "buy every favourite" in 9 of 9 cells, +0.030..+0.053.

  HIGH is negative 9 of 9 here; V called high "mixed".
  So the two samples agree on the LOW end and disagree on the SHAPE. V's pattern was non-monotone
  (low +, mid --, high ~) and flagged as the main caution; mine is cleanly MONOTONE low > mid > high.
  A monotone vol effect is the more credible of the two, but the disagreement is real and is not
  resolved by these days - it is the thing a third sample should settle.

REGIME DIFFERENCE WORTH SAYING FIRST
  On 09-24..28 buying the favourite was profitable OUTRIGHT (+0.051/$1 with no vol filter); on V's
  09-13..16 the same "all" cells were mostly negative. My dollar totals are therefore larger than V's
  for reasons that have nothing to do with the rule. The low-vol filter still roughly DOUBLES per$1
  over the unfiltered null and passes the null gate - but the gap between our headline $ figures is
  regime, not edge, and should not be read as the rule being twice as good as V measured.

within one tick 74.7% (median hold 1 s) - a moving book, not a forward-filled one. London's real 31%
is for EF fires, which by construction fire on a transient DIP and often have nothing to hit; buying a
resting favourite is a different situation, so a much higher fill is expected rather than suspicious.
