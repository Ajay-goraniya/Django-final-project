   the exact OPPOSITE of what the same filter did to FAV taker entries in M33 this morning, where
   the candles it threw away paid five times more than the ones it kept.
   THE TWO RESULTS ARE CONSISTENT AND THE MECHANISM IS THE ONE THIS PROJECT KEEPS FINDING: a maker
   entry is ADVERSELY SELECTED - a resting bid only trades when the ask comes down to it, i.e. when
   the side is going wrong. Requiring Binance to be moving our way at that moment is precisely a
   test of whether the fill is adverse or benign, so it has something to remove. A taker entry at
   the ask has no such selection, so the same filter only removes good trades with the bad.

   BUT I WILL NOT CALL IT A PASS, FOR ONE SPECIFIC REASON BEYOND THE 75% FILL FIDELITY: the fill
   model's trigger IS the adverse event. We "fill" exactly when the ask falls to our bid, and the
   filter is a momentum test at that same instant - so kept-vs-removed may be partly a split on the
   fill model's own error rather than on the market. A filter that looks good against a fill rule it
   is correlated with is the shape of a false positive, and this box cannot separate the two: M30's
   gate failed at 75.4%, so the fills are approximate by one in four.
   THE ONLY TEST THAT SETTLES IT IS PART (b) - London's REAL maker fills since 10-02 15:04, where
   the fills are facts rather than a model. Part (a) says the hypothesis is worth that test; it
   cannot say more, and I am not proposing a live change on it.
