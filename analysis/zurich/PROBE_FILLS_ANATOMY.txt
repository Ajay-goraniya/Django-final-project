A. THE TAPE CANNOT DECIDE TOUCH vs THRU AT THE TIMES WE TRADE, so neither sim number is earned.
   Our orders live for a MEDIAN OF 3 SECONDS (p10 0 s, p90 214 s). In that window the tape shows
   "neither" for 77 of 93 filled orders - not because nothing traded through us, but because a
   1-minute REST cron with per-second stamps cannot resolve a 3-second interval, and our OWN fill is
   absent from it for 8 of 93 filled orders. Widen the window to the whole candle and the
   classification collapses the other way: 147 of 157 orders are "thru", 87 of 93 fills among them.
   So at candle granularity everything is thru and at second granularity nothing is: the public tape
   has no resolution where the distinction lives. STRICT_PFAV_CALM's touch (+155/20d) versus thru
   (-335/20d) is therefore a choice between two numbers computed on data blind to our actual fills,
   and the -335 should not be treated as a refutation of the live rule. What WOULD settle it is our
   own order-level data: queue position and partial-fill timing from the venue, not the public tape.

B. A PRICE STOP DOES NOT CUT THE LOSERS PROFITABLY. On all 97 graded fills every level loses:
   -39.37 at 0.50, -38.38 at 0.40, -45.09 at 0.30, -49.13 at 0.20, -49.07 at 0.10, because 40
   WINNERS dipped to 0.50 before recovering and each stop gives up shares*(1-L).
   Excluding the 09-30 17:00 candle it flips to roughly break-even: -5.73, +1.72, +1.37, +3.60 and
   +9.84 at L=0.10, where no winner ever traded that low and all 21 losers are caught.
   READ IT HONESTLY: a stop only helps once you remove the single candle that produced most of the
   probe's profit, and that candle is precisely where winners went deep before coming back - a stop
   would have destroyed the probe's best day. The one cell that is both positive and cheap (L=0.10,
   +9.84 over 82 fills, 0 winners stopped) is worth about 12 cents a fill and rests entirely on 21
   losers in two days. It is not a recommendation; it is the only level that would not have cut
   winners, and it should be re-measured over a month before anyone touches the probe.
   The exit price here is EVIDENCE a bid existed (a maker-BUY print), not a bid path we could have
   hit at our size - an upper bound on how well a stop could have worked.
