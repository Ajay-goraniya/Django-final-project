IS THE GATE RE-CHECKED WHILE RESTING? No. decide() tests vol only in the "nothing resting: may we
post?" branch. A resting order leaves on exactly two things: Binance >= 2 bps adverse, or sec outside
60-180. Nothing re-reads vol, so a LIVE vol could rise after a post and the order would stay - but on
this order the gate value itself never moved, so that is not what happened here.
WHAT DID HAPPEN: Binance moved 2.34 bps AGAINST us and the fill landed on the same pass as the cancel
decision - fills.bn_before_bps = 2.3372, log "filled before cancel (adverse 2.34bps)". That is the
known fill-before-cancel race, which the probe handles correctly (it books the fill instead of
cancelling). It is not a calm-gate breach.
