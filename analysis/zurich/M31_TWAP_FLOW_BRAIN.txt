FOUR VARIANTS TRIED, FOUR FAILURES. Counting them as the prereg demands: four tries is four chances
of luck, and none of them took it. The bar was a logloss gain AND paired p<0.05; V1 and V4 failed
the gain outright, V2 and V3 got a gain of about a quarter of a percent while moving 7 and 2 side
calls out of 541 respectively. That is calibration polish, not information - and a feature that
changes two decisions in 541 cannot be traded whatever its logloss does.
V1 was also run on the FIRST three days before amendment 2, with the same answer and a wider margin:
M0 0.2956, M1 0.3167, M2 0.3592. So order flow failed on the easy days and the hard days alike.

THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MORE THAN THE FOUR FAILURES: M1 DOES NOT BEAT M0.
On the hard days, market mid alone is 0.3407 / 86.69% and M1 (+ all the TWAP lock-in math) is
0.3410 / 86.69% - a hair WORSE, and identical on side accuracy. On the first three days M0 was
clearly better: 0.2956 against 0.3167. So the TWAP math adds nothing to the venue's own price.
This is the real content of M31, and it reframes the whole exercise: the prereg set the bar at
"beat M1", but M1 is not actually better than the quoted mid, so every variant was in effect being
asked to beat the market price - and V's own rule for this study was "test only inputs that can know
something the market price doesn't." The answer on 541 out-of-sample rows is that by second 240-280
the ask already contains the lock-in arithmetic. Of the candle's 300 seconds, 240 are history by
then and 48 of the 60 TWAP seconds are already fixed; it would be surprising if a market this liquid
had NOT priced that, and it has.
So the honest conclusion is not "order flow is useless". It is that the decision seconds the prereg
fixed are too late for ANY of these inputs to add anything: the market has already done the sum.
If this line of work continues, the question to put to the owner is whether to decide EARLIER - at
60-180 s, where the remaining TWAP window is genuinely unresolved and a flow signal would have
something left to predict - rather than to keep testing new inputs at 240-280 s. I am not proposing
that as a new arm here; M31 as specified is finished and it is a FAIL.
