THE ARITHMETIC THAT DECIDES THIS, AND IT IS NOT A SAMPLE EFFECT: at sec 180 the mean
ask_UP + ask_DOWN is 1.0105. Buying BOTH sides therefore pays 1.0105 for a position that pays out
exactly 1.00 whatever happens - the two legs are complementary, one settles at 1 and the other at 0.
So the trade starts 1.05 cents in the hole per dollar before a single stop is considered, and the
Polymarket taker fee on both legs takes it to -0.0274. That is the floor the rule has to beat:
  HOLD BOTH SIDES, no stops, fee   -0.0274 per $1, 0 of 12 green days
  HOLD BOTH SIDES, no stops, 0 fee -0.0103 per $1, 0 of 12 green days
