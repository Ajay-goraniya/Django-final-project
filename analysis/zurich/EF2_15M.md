# EF2_15M — is the venue's price as good on the SLOWER market?

Zurich, 2026-09-28. READ-ONLY. **Master OFF, London keeps fixed15.** v0b dropped on the owner's ruling that
early fires are guessing; v1/v2 dropped as prediction models on btc5.

## Three corrections to the brief, before any numbers

1. **btc15 books do not go back to 09-22.** The recorder holds **09-27 03:30 → 09-28 11:30, 32.0 h**, 131
   markets, **126 gamma-resolved candles**. Everything here is on those 126.
2. **There is no eth15 or sol15.** `recorder.py` records ETH and SOL on the 5-minute grid and only BTC on the
   15-minute grid, so the ETH/SOL 15m comparison has no data behind it at all.
3. **(c), the 52-feature walk-forward, is not viable and I have not faked it.** 126 independent outcomes
   cannot support 52 features. Worse, the engine's 44 features are computed against the *5m* line and the *5m*
   book, so transplanting them to a 15m decision needs `imb5`, `imb20`, `spread_bps` and `micro_bps` zeroed —
   which is precisely the level-shift that inverted the London 35-key run an hour ago. What is delivered
   instead is (b): the same *family* of spot features, rebuilt against the 15m line with `sec_left` scaled to
   900, and **fitted on the 15m data**, so nothing is transplanted and nothing is imputed.

**The slower market's price is not softer.** Past a third of the way through the candle the 15m ask is
*better* than the 5m ask at the matching fraction. It is worse only in the first fifth, and the reason is
visible in the last column: **the 15m ask sits at 0.505 for the first several minutes** — the market has not
formed a view yet, so there is nothing for it to be right about.

That flat early book is the only place a model could have room, and (b) is the test of whether it does.

**Two of seven are positive, the sign flips with no pattern in S, and not one delta reaches even 1.1 standard
errors.** The largest, +0.075 at S=120, is 1.03 SE — exactly the size of the noise. On the 5m market the full
52-feature model beat the ask by +0.003; here the honest answer is that nothing is measurably different from
zero in either direction.

The walk-forward has **one training day**. btc15 covers 09-27 and 09-28, so every row above is trained on
09-27 and tested on 09-28. None of it is offered as a finding.

## Why there is no disagreement-cell grid

V's instruction was conditional: report the disagreement cells *"if the model beats the ask by more than it
does on 5m"*. It does not — nothing clears one standard error. Running the grid anyway would mean slicing 86
held-out rows into `p >= 0.60 & ask <= 0.50` style cells that would hold single digits each, and then reading
a per-$1 off them. That is the cherry-pick this whole document has been avoiding, so it is not here. If V
wants it regardless, say so and it is ten minutes.

## Answer

**The price is just as good on the slower market.** Past the first fifth of the candle the 15m ask is at
least as informative as the 5m ask at the same stage, and a model fitted on 15m's own data does not beat it
anywhere by a margin distinguishable from noise. The early-candle window where the 15m book is uninformative
(ask pinned at 0.505, AUC 0.60) is exactly the window the owner has ruled out as gambling — and on this
evidence he is right to: the model is no better there than the flat book is, scoring 0.467 and 0.486 at
S=30 and S=60 against a coin.

The 15-minute market is not a softer venue. It is the same market on a longer clock.
