WHY PFAV AND PFAV_THRU ARE THE SAME ROW TODAY - checked, not assumed.
Identical numbers across two arms is the pattern this project keeps getting wrong, so I compared
them row by row rather than publishing the coincidence. Both arms are live and written separately
(each has 432 rows, both last written 10-02 00:45). On 10-01 they agree on 262 of 262 epochs: the
trade-through condition - the print must go STRICTLY below our bid, not merely touch it - removed
ZERO fills that day. On 09-30 it removed 3 of 162 (12:15, 17:10, 22:15, each a 0.80 bid that filled
for PFAV and did not fill for THRU). So the filter is real but nearly inert: 3 differing rows in 424.
That is worth knowing on its own - PFAV_THRU was built to be the pessimistic twin of PFAV, and on
this box's forward data it is not meaningfully more pessimistic, so it cannot be used as evidence
that PFAV's fills are achievable. The honest reading stays the one from 09-30: both are deeply
negative, -93.62 today and -117.58 once each trade pays a cent more.
