FAV UNDER AN ARRIVAL-BASED FILL  (owner: "be sure about fills at the time the order reaches Polymarket")
========================================================================================================
Model, V's rule implemented literally: cap = decision ask + 1 tick; FILL only if the same-side ask at
decision + LAG (the next decide_log pass at or AFTER that instant, never an earlier one) is <= cap AND
the size at that level covers our shares; pay that arrival ask, fee-exact. Forward arm uses +500 ms.

HEADLINE. Judging the fill at arrival rather than at decision roughly HALVES the edge:
    1 s book model (the parity grid that validated the rule)   +0.043 / $1
    arrival +500 ms, this model                                +0.019 / $1
and the earlier +0.110 figure from a 250 ms in-sample replay should be ignored entirely.

The SHAPE survives, which is the part that matters for the registration:
    FAV (low vol)  +0.019      FAV_mid  +0.008      FAV_hi  -0.029      FAV_all  +0.005
Low still beats mid, high is still the only clearly negative bucket, and low still beats the
unfiltered null. So arrival pricing costs edge without inverting the finding.

SENSITIVITY. fill% falls monotonically with lag - 88.4 / 84.8 / 78.9 - exactly as it should, because a
later book has had more time to move away. But the DOLLARS are NOT monotone (+162.0 / +119.7 / +165.4)
while win% of fills barely moves (75.6 / 74.9 / 75.2). That non-monotonicity is noise, not a signal
about latency, and it is the reason to keep V's +500 ms rather than read +1000 as "better". Anyone
picking the best-looking lag here would be fitting to 644 decisions of chop.

THE SIZE GATE IS THE WEAK PART, and it is a data limit, not a modelling choice. Polymarket ask SIZES for
the btc5 market exist only from 09-28 18:13, when the book recorder started: 67 candles, 5.5 h. So:
  * the PRICE condition is measured across the whole FAV backfill (1,200 decisions);
  * the SIZE condition is measurable on 30 FAV fires -> 24 pass, 6 rejected. INSUFFICIENT (n < 60).
  * WORTH WATCHING: that is a 20% rejection rate, against only 3.8% of all btc5 book rows holding less
    than the ~14 shares $10 buys at 0.72. If that holds up it means the book is thinner at the moments
    FAV wants to trade than at random moments - i.e. the size gate bites where it hurts. On n=30 that
    is a hypothesis, not a result, and forward rows will settle it. If it does hold, FAV's effective
    fill drops from ~85% to ~68% and the +0.019 above is optimistic.

Shares for the gate are 10/paid, V's literal words. This is STRICTER than the fee-exact 10/be(paid),
since be(paid) > paid, so the gate demands more size than the order would actually consume. Left strict
deliberately: a fill gate should err toward not filling.

FORWARD WIRING. FAV/FAV_mid/FAV_all now use FAV_LAG_MS = 500 plus the size gate. This is deliberately
NOT the shared DELAY_MS: that stays 250 ms for arms A-F, because changing it would silently re-grade
ten already-registered arms. Verified rather than assumed - on 286 FAV decisions the arrival fill
differs from the shared 250 ms fill in 88 of them (53 a different price, 31 no-fill where 250 ms filled,
4 the reverse), so the new path is demonstrably live; and the shared row is mutated in 0 of 286, so
arms A-F keep their own fill. ef3_shadow.py --selftest still row-for-row against ef2_rows.npz.

Where the recorder has no book row for that second the size cannot be checked and the fill is allowed
on price alone. btc5 sizes begin 09-28 18:13 and FAV's first forward row is 09-28 23:30, so every
forward row has a real size check; only pre-recorder history could ever be unchecked.
