WHAT THE NUMBERS ACTUALLY SAY
 1. ACCRUAL IS FINE. On the 8-day historical not-calm share (46.0%) the arm should take ~119 fills a
    day, putting n=100 at 10-07 and n=200 at 10-08. Even on the pessimistic rate observed so far
    (16.1%, i.e. assuming the quiet window repeats forever) it is ~42 fills a day, n=100 on 10-09 and
    n=200 on 10-11. Either way the early-stop check at n>=100 and the n=200 verdict are both days
    away, not unreachable. My "effectively unreachable" line should be struck.
 2. THE SAMPLING SKEW IS REAL THOUGH, and it is the part worth keeping. Not-calm share runs from
    12.5% at 21:00Z to 79.2% at 15:00Z - a 6x swing across the day, with a clear block of high hours
    12:00-16:00Z (59.4 / 64.6 / 75.0 / 79.2 / 68.8%) and a clear trough 20:00-23:00Z. So M53-NC's
    fills WILL cluster in the European afternoon / US morning and under-sample the late-evening and
    early-Asia hours. That is not a defect - it is what the frozen filter does - but n=200 will be a
    sample of those hours rather than of the day, and the verdict should be read with that in view.
 3. THE FILTER IS ALSO A VOLATILITY-REGIME FILTER ACROSS DAYS, not just hours: 09-29..10-02 ran
    58.7-70.8% not-calm, then 10-03 collapsed to 4.9% and 10-04 to 16.0%, then 10-05 recovered to
    55.2%. Those are the same two days the FAV dailies flagged as the quiet-market outlier (10-03
    BTC range 0.69%). So the arm's fill rate will swing with the regime as well as the clock, and a
    week that happens to contain another 10-03 will accrue much more slowly.
 4. NO DATA GAP: 0 of 2,304 candles over the eight days lacked a vol reading, so the frozen >=240 of
    300 seconds requirement is not the constraint. The 2 skipped-no-data rows in the arm's own log
    were my stale-cache defect (fixed, 5115572), not a property of the feed.
 5. FILL QUALITY, for completeness: 14 of 14 not-calm signals filled FULL, 0 partial, 0 missed, so
    at a $5 stake level-1 depth has not yet bound. 90% of candles produce a qualifying print, so the
    not-calm share - not the signal rule - is what sets the pace.
