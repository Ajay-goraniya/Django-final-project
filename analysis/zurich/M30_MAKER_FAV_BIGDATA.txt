WHY THE PRINT MODELS FAIL, measured and not guessed: the probe's own 108 real fills appear in
tape_btc5 only 38 times - 70 ARE SIMPLY NOT IN THE TAPE - even though the tape carries a median
of 2,324 prints per candle. So the tape is dense but incomplete for small fills (ours were 5
shares, about $3), and a fill model built on it cannot see two thirds of its own ground truth.
This is the same artefact the 10-02 anatomy study hit ("77 of 93 fills with no print").
The book model misses differently: 16 real fills it calls no-fill and 12 no-fills it calls fills.

not going to soften that by quoting the two conditions it did pass.

So on 14 days and 1,771 fires, FAV's +2.2 cents is NOT a demonstrated edge - it is a positive
number that a sample this size cannot separate from zero. What IS demonstrated, at better than
2 SE in both cases, is that the two arms the calm gate EXCLUDES lose money. That is the real
result of M30: the 0.304 cut is not decoration. It is removing a significant loss, and the
monotone shape ef3_shadow was built to watch forward (FAV > FAV_mid, and FAV_all the null in
between) survives 14 days of unseen data in the right order. What the cut does not do, on this
evidence, is turn the remainder into a provable profit.

ROBUSTNESS, since a single window can flatter a rule:
 - BOTH ask sources agree on the sign and roughly the size: 09-08..09-16 venues.q +0.0232 (n 789),
   09-22..10-01 tape1s +0.0204 (n 982). Two different instruments, two different grids, same answer -
   which is the main reason I believe the FAV number is real even though it is not significant.
 - The +1c cost test cuts FAV from +0.0216 to +0.0082, so roughly 60% of the gross edge is one tick.
   A rule whose edge is smaller than a tick and a half is not a rule to size up.
 - Taking the ask at the NEXT sample instead of the decision sample barely moves it (+0.0226), so
   the result does not depend on the sub-second execution assumption I could not resolve.

ON MAKER, AND WHY I AM NOT REPORTING A NUMBER AS IF IT MEANT SOMETHING:
Both fill models the prereg specifies are built on tape_btc5 prints, and that input provably cannot
see its own ground truth - the probe's 108 real fills appear in the tape 38 times. 70 are absent,
despite a median 2,324 prints per candle. The book-based model I added reaches 75.4%, still short
of 85%. On the strength of a 75%-accurate fill model the MAKER grid (+0.0235 per $1, t +0.59, 3 of
5 green days) carries no weight in either direction, and the right conclusion is that THIS BOX
CANNOT CURRENTLY TEST THE MAKER RULE OFF-LINE. To change that, the fix is not more analysis - it is
recording our own resting-order state and the top of book at 1 Hz while the rule runs live, which
is what the probe did for 88 candles and is the only fill evidence here that proved reliable.
One more thing the grid does say, for what it is worth at this n: the no-vol-gate null returns
+0.1122 per $1 against the gated rule's +0.1649 on the same print model, so the calm gate improves
the maker leg per dollar too - the same direction the FAV arms show, from independent data.
