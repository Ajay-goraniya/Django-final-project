BINANCE CLOCK QUESTION - SETTLED 10-02 01:5x UTC. READ-ONLY, cheap, no credentials.

WHAT I CLAIMED EARLIER AND GOT WRONG
At 01:38 I told V that Binance's REST serverTime read +329 ms ahead of NTP with a 4 ms spread and
that "path asymmetry cannot explain it at an 80-130 ms round trip", so the true Binance feed lag
might be ~440 ms rather than 112 ms and the realistic arm might be D=700 rather than D=300.
That reasoning was wrong. The 80-130 ms figure was a curl round trip; the python urllib round trip
to that same host is ~883 ms, because urllib does a fresh TLS handshake per request and that host
is slow. At an 883 ms round trip the midpoint estimate can be biased by hundreds of ms, so the
offset was MY measurement artefact, not Binance's clock.

A) SEVERAL INDEPENDENT BINANCE HOSTS, serverTime minus our NTP-exact clock (chrony RMS 1.6 us)
  host                      skew p50    own rtt p50   skew spread
  data-api.binance.vision     +331 ms      883 ms         20 ms
  api-gcp.binance.com         +384 ms     1026 ms        382 ms
  api.binance.com               +3 ms      227 ms          2 ms
  fapi.binance.com              +4 ms      229 ms          1 ms
  testnet.binance.vision        +4 ms      227 ms        103 ms
The apparent offset TRACKS THE ROUND TRIP: the three fast-path hosts agree with our clock to within
3-4 ms, the two slow-path hosts appear hundreds of ms ahead. That is the signature of an asymmetric
path, not of a skewed exchange clock.

B) THE DECISIVE TEST, which needs no second opinion about anyone's clock
A 1 s kline cannot be PUBLISHED before it has CLOSED. So (our receipt) minus (its own close time)
must be positive by at least the network lag. If Binance's publishing clock ran ~330 ms ahead of
real time, a bar stamped close=X would be emitted at real time X-0.33 s and we would receive it
BEFORE its own close time by our clock.
  btcusdt@kline_1s, closed bars only, n 76 over 75 s:
    receipt - kline CLOSE time : p50 +139 ms   p10 +127   p90 +166
    receipt - stream EVENT time: p50 +111 ms   p10 +110   p90 +112
Every bar arrived AFTER its close time, p10 included. The publishing clock is not ahead.

CONCLUSION
The Binance market-data clock agrees with ours. The true feed lag is the directly measured ~111 ms
(p90 ~126 ms), and the REST serverTime offset is a property of two slow REST hosts, not of the
market-data path. It must NOT be added to the reaction-delay budget.
So the D verdict stands as first stated: D=300 is reachable on the feed side; D=700 remains the
conservative planning number only because the SIGNED WRITE leg is still unmeasured (the CLOB read
round trip, p50 43 ms, is a floor on it). Nothing here needs D=1000 or D=1500.
