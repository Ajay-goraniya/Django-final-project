WHY THIS RE-RUN CANNOT ACCUMULATE, WHICH MATTERS MORE THAN THE FAIL
The standing job is framed as "as soon as data allows". On the current source it never will:
 * stable_ef.py rebuilds fires from the engine's `diagnostics` pnl rows. That table spans
   09-30 12:00 .. 10-07 12:34 ONLY - the engine PRUNES it on a rolling ~7-day window. (The script's
   "10 days" counts 339 junk rows stamped 01-01 plus two partial days; the honest figure is ~7.)
 * So n in the top-20% tier sits at 31 against the 60 bar and is a STEADY STATE, not a partial
   sample. Waiting another week leaves it at ~31.
 * The hourly archive (pm_archive/zurich_research_archive.sqlite3), built precisely to outlive these
   prunes, holds decide_log back to 09-24 - about 13 days, roughly double the window. Rebuilding
   fires from decide_log instead of diagnostics would plausibly take the top-20% tier to ~60.
   That is a DATA-SOURCE change to a frozen script, so I am not making it unilaterally.
 * Related, same cause: the `grading provenance` gate failed with "sources share no keys" because
   BTC5 = /tmp/poly/btc5.sqlite3 is the gamma mirror from SEP 27 and shares no epochs with the
   09-30+ window. Grading therefore fell back to engine results.actual on 223 of 271 fires. A fresh
   mirror exists at pm_ef3/gamma_zurich.sqlite3 with the identical schema and would fix that gate -
   again a source change, again not mine to make.
