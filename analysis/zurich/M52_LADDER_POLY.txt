  VERDICT: FAIL on every cell. Nothing passes with fees; nothing is distinguishable from zero without.
  1. S60 is the only survivable shape: -0.038/$ with fees, -0.0015/$ at zero fee (t -0.32) - i.e. the
     ladder at zero fee is exactly nothing, and the fee is the whole loss. x2.00 is worse than x1.00
     per candle (-0.1556 vs -0.0971) while looking better per $ staked (-0.0349 vs -0.0383): the same
     martingale illusion as M50B - the big stakes land on the re-buys that run, and it stakes 3x more.
  2. THE LADDER LOSES TO DOING NOTHING. Paired on the identical entries, S60 ladder minus hold is
     -0.0706/candle (t -7.01) with fees and -0.0158 (t -1.66) at zero fee, better on only 30% of
     candles. The stops are not protection here, they are a cost: each one sells 10c below the level
     into the bid and pays the taker fee twice, and the re-buy needs the price back 10c higher.
  3. B60/B80 are structurally dead before the rule runs: mean combined ask 1.0137 / 1.0127 for a payout
     of exactly 1.00 = -0.0135 / -0.0125 per $ staked, guaranteed, regardless of what the ladder does.
  4. THE ONE THING THE RULE DOES DELIVER is what the owner asked for and it is not edge: B60 x2.00 at
     zero fee wins on 63% of candles and beats hold on 74% of them, yet the paired mean is only
     +0.0156/candle (t +0.53) and the worst candle is -18.41. More consistency, same expectancy, a
     much fatter tail. With fees that same cell is -0.2565/candle.
  5. The zero-fee S60 hold reference is +0.0084/$ at t +1.32 - a fifth price level at which the
     5-minute favourite is fairly priced, consistent with M44 (+0.0022 at 0.60), M47/M49 (+0.0021 at
     0.70) and M50B (+0.0005 at 0.80). The entry is fair, so no stop/re-buy/stake schedule built on
     top of it can be better than fair minus costs - which is what all 14 cells show.
