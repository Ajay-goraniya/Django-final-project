VERDICT (Zurich, 10-02 13:2x). YES ON EXECUTION - AND THE PROBE PAYS FOR IT IN SELECTION.
Both effects are real, they pull opposite ways, and reporting only the first would be the flattering
half of the answer.
1. EXECUTION IS WORTH ABOUT +0.18 PER $1 ON IDENTICAL CANDLES. On the 70 candles where both filled,
   the outcome is the same by construction (same candle, same venue resolution), the side agrees on
   67 of 70, and the probe still returns +0.0846/$1 against FAV's -0.0929/$1. Paired per candle the
   probe is better on 47 of 49 non-tied candles, mean +0.1208/$1, exact sign-flip p 0.0000. The
   mechanism is exactly what a maker is: the probe pays a mean bid of 0.6934 where the taker's
   fee-exact cost is 0.7351 - 4.2 cents a share, of which the raw ask accounts for 3.4 and the fee
   the rest - and it enters 9 s later (sec 78 vs 69) rather than earlier. Nothing about the rule
   differs on these candles. This is the clean answer to the question asked.
2. BUT THE PROBE'S FILLS ARE ADVERSELY SELECTED, and the size of it is comparable. On the 89 candles
   FAV traded and the probe did NOT get filled, FAV went 72W/17L for +0.1113/$1 - that is where the
   taker made its money. On the 70 shared candles the taker LOST (-0.0929/$1). So the candles the
   probe cannot reach are the good ones, and the candles where somebody is willing to sell into a
   resting bid are disproportionately the ones that go wrong. A passive quote only trades when
   someone wants the other side, and that want is information.
3. NET, the probe is still positive: +0.0846/$1 on the shared candles and +0.0417/$1 on the 18
   candles FAV skipped entirely (12W/6L, ask outside 0.65-0.85 or a gate FAV applies), so the
   execution advantage more than covers the selection penalty ON WHAT IT ACTUALLY TRADES. What it
   does NOT do is capture the +95.56 the taker made on the missed set. Both statements belong in any
   proposal: passive execution is better per candle, and passive reach is worse.
4. TODAY 10-02, the bad day, says the same thing more sharply: on the 28 shared candles the probe is
   -0.0854/$1 against FAV's -0.2514/$1, better on 18 of 18 non-tied candles (mean +0.1779/$1), so
   execution cushioned a losing day rather than created a winning one. The missed set today is only
   7 candles (+0.0774/$1 for FAV) and probe-only 7 (+0.1338/$1), both far too small to read.

ONE ASYMMETRY TO KEEP IN MIND: the probe's numbers are REAL money with real fills, FAV's are a
simulation with an arrival-fill model whose shares are a floor. That cuts in the probe's favour on
reliability and against it on size - FAV's deployed capital is larger per candle ($679 over 70
candles against the probe's $280) because the probe's 5 shares are a smaller stake than FAV's $10.
Per $1 is the only fair comparison and it is what the verdict uses.
