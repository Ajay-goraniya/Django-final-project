ANSWER TO THE OWNER'S QUESTION - "does this happen all the time?"  NO. IT REVERSES.

London's real fixed15, 09-23..10-01, n=366:          00-13  -235      13-24  +151
Zurich fixed15 on the days NOT in that window
(09-15, 09-16, 09-21, 09-22), London execution:      00-13  +23.44    13-24  -130.64
                                                     (+0.105/$1)      (-0.350/$1)
Both halves come out with the OPPOSITE SIGN to London's. The morning, which lost 235 on London's
days, made money on the out-of-window days and won 2 of the 3 days that had any morning fires. The
afternoon, which made 151 on London's days, is the worst bucket here at -0.405/$1 in US 13-20.

ON THE OVERLAPPING DAYS I REPRODUCE LONDON. Inside 09-23..10-01 the morning lost on 7 of 7 days with
morning fires (-107.67 all told). So Zurich's data and London's agree about 09-23..10-01 and disagree
about everything before it. That is the shape of a WINDOW effect, not a clock effect: the thing being
measured is those nine days, not the hours of the day.

SO THE HONEST READING IS NOT "the morning is good after all". It is that the morning/afternoon split
is NOT STABLE, and neither sign should be traded. Two independent reasons to refuse it:
  1. SAMPLE. Every bucket in the independent sample is UNDER 60 FILLS - 19, 18, 46 and 14 - and only
     3 days carry any morning fires at all. A reversal on 37 morning fills is not evidence of a
     reversal; it is evidence that 37 fills cannot settle the question.
  2. EXECUTION. Pooled over all 12 days at London execution BOTH halves lose: 00-13 -107.67
     (-0.173/$1) and 13-24 -163.20 (-0.204/$1), with 8 of 10 morning days losing. Whatever the clock
     is doing, fixed15 at London's execution is negative in both halves once the days are pooled -
     which is the same conclusion the stable-EF re-run reached this morning by a different route.

WHAT WOULD ACTUALLY ANSWER IT: the same split on London's OWN longer history, before 09-23, with the
buckets fixed in advance. Zurich's EF history only reaches back to 09-15 and is thin before 09-21, so
4 usable out-of-window days is all this box can offer. I am not proposing a session rule either way.
