## 3. My Task 6 weekend conclusion largely FAILS — correcting it

| | 71 days | **252 days** |
|---|---|---|
| Mon-Fri t20 | 58.6% | **58.3%** (n=51,792) |
| Sat-Sun t20 | **61.1%** | **58.4%** (n=20,539) |
| gap | **+2.5** | **+0.1** |
| Mon-Fri t60 | 64.8% | 64.7% |
| Sat-Sun t60 | 65.9% | **63.7%** |

**"Weekends are more predictable" does not survive.** Early accuracy is flat (58.3 vs 58.4), and at
t=60 and t=190 weekends are now slightly *worse* (63.7 vs 64.7; 77.5 vs 79.7). The +2.5pp weekend
edge I reported was a 71-day artefact.

The volatility mechanism weakens with it. On 252 days, t=20 accuracy by range quartile is
**58.7 / 58.9 / 58.1 / 57.7** — a 1.2pp spread, not the 3.7pp I reported — and at t=60 it now
*increases* with volatility (63.4 → 65.9), the opposite of what I told V.

**What survives:** the structural regime difference is real and large. Weekend candles cross the
open **2.96 times vs 4.10**, and their range is **10.9 vs 17.7 bps**. Weekends are genuinely
quieter and less choppy — they are just not more predictable.

**What this means for the user's original question:** the answer is unchanged in substance and
actually cleaner. There is **no weekday-to-weekend decay in the signal** — it is flat, not better,
not worse. So any real weekend losses in their older models still point at sizing, EV thresholds and
fills calibrated on 17.7 bps candles being applied to 10.9 bps ones, not at the direction model.

**Correction to what I sent V at 12:58:** I said early accuracy falls monotonically with volatility
and that this was "the whole story" behind the rv60 gate reversing between the v10 and v11 paths.
On 252 days that effect is 1.2pp and non-monotone, which is too weak to carry that explanation.
V had already refuted the vol gate empirically on the twins, so nothing live rested on it — but the
mechanism I offered is withdrawn. The honest position is that the v10/v11 rv60 disagreement remains
unexplained.

## Method note

This is the third time today a finding has shrunk or reversed when the sample grew or the parameter
range was swept — V's trend guard, my entry cap, and now my weekend result. The pattern is
consistent: **effects measured on one day of fires or ten weeks of candles are not yet effects.**
Everything I report from here carries its n and gets re-run when the sample grows.
