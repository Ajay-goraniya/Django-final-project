# H1 — Task 11.3: the second, later EF entry

**This is the strongest result of the day and the only one that is not a threshold on a weak score.
It survives every robustness check I ran. It is also large enough that I do not fully believe it yet
and V should reproduce it on a live twin before anything ships.**

## The construction

Not a gate. EF's direction is taken as given — its own call, fixed at ~20 s. The question is whether
**buying more of that same side later in the candle, at the price the venue is showing then**, pays.

Data: `venues.sqlite3.gz` `q` table — the per-second Predict.fun ask path (616 candles, 37,079 rows),
joined to the `outcome` table for settlement and to EF fires from the twins and the v10 engine.
**347 distinct candles**, each counted once. PnL at $1 with the 2% fee.

## Result

| t | rule | fires | hit | PnL | **per-fire** | avg ask | h1 | h2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 60 | any ask | 346 | 59% | +84.74 | +0.245 | 0.50 | +54.93 | +29.81 |
| 60 | cap 0.60 | 255 | 54% | +82.63 | +0.324 | 0.43 | +58.89 | +23.74 |
| **120** | any ask | 346 | 59% | +87.90 | +0.254 | 0.51 | +40.88 | +47.01 |
| **120** | **cap 0.60** | **215** | 48% | **+87.69** | **+0.408** | 0.36 | **+44.20** | **+43.49** |
| 190 | cap 0.60 | 206 | 39% | +36.66 | +0.178 | 0.30 | +28.76 | +7.90 |
| 240 | cap 0.60 | 179 | 26% | −29.65 | −0.166 | 0.20 | −8.28 | −21.37 |

For scale: **EF live runs about +0.03 to +0.10 per fire.** This is +0.408.

## Where the money comes from — and it is not accuracy

Note the hit rate **falls** as the cap tightens: 59% → 48% → 39% → 34%. Requiring the ask to still be
≤0.60 selects candles where **the market disagrees with EF**. That is adverse selection, and it still
pays, because **the price falls faster than the accuracy does.** At t=120 with a 0.60 cap the average
ask is 0.36 (implied break-even ~36.7% with the fee) and the side wins 48%.

The claim is therefore a mispricing claim, not a forecasting one: **when the venue marks EF's side
down late in the candle, it over-discounts it.** That is a claim about the book, not about our model —
which is why it does not run into the wall that killed everything else today.

## Robustness — all four checks pass

**1. The prices are asks, not bids.** `pred_up + pred_dn` has median **1.010** (p10 1.010, p90 1.020),
i.e. the pair carries a ~1c spread above par. If these were bids the sum would sit below 1.0.

**2. The cap sweep is monotone, with no spike:** per-fire +0.254 (no cap) → +0.281 (0.80) → +0.322
(0.70) → +0.408 (0.60) → +0.481 (0.50) → +0.553 (0.40) → +0.769 (0.30). Total PnL stays flat (+87.90
→ +87.69 → +80.88 → +68.63) as the fire count falls. That is the smooth-regularity signature, the
opposite of the trend guard's spike.

**3. Both halves positive at every cap** — +44.20/+43.49 at 0.60, balanced rather than h1-loaded. Every
other candidate today failed exactly here.

**4. Slippage stress:** +0.408 at the quote, +0.355 at +1c, +0.307 at +2c, +0.186 at +5c, **+0.035
even at +10c**. Tokyo's real fills average +0.41c worse than quote, which would land near +0.386.

**5. Liquidity is adequate**, and far better than REVERSAL's late book: size on our side at t=120
median **129**, p25 48 (REVERSAL's 190 s book was median 30, p25 15.6).

## Why I still do not fully believe it

+0.408 per fire against EF live's +0.03–0.10 is a 4–13× improvement on a liquid book, and markets do
not usually leave that lying around. Everything I found today that looked this good shrank when
tested harder. Specific things that could still be wrong:

- **Quote staleness.** The `q` rows are ~5 s apart and I match within ±7 s. If a printed ask is stale
  relative to a fast move, the fill would not have been available at that price.
- **Size at that exact second**, not just the size column's headline.
- **347 candles over ~2 days, one venue.** The cap sweep and halves are internally consistent but the
  window is short and there is only one regime in it.
- **Selection:** only candles where an EF fire and a quote path both exist.

## Recommendation

**Run it as a shadow twin immediately — a second EF entry at t≈120 s, same side, only if that side's
ask is ≤0.60 — and grade it against the live book.** That is the cheapest way to find out whether the
recorded quote path is tradeable. Do not put it on Tokyo until a twin reproduces it on live quotes.

If it survives, the interesting part is that it is additive rather than a replacement: EF's first fire
keeps its own economics, and this adds a second position on the same conviction at a much better
price.

## Caveats

347 distinct candles; 215 kept at the 0.60 cap at t=120, so the ≥100 bar is met. Halves split by
candle. Fees at 2%, no fill model beyond the slippage stress above. EF direction taken from the
twins/engine record, so this inherits whatever selection their fire rule imposes.
