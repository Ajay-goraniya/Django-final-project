8. L1 MODEL DESIGN
======================================================================

Offset residual logistic over the winning L0, prequential, ridge, walk-
forward, train-only standardisation. Agreed.

THE PUSH-BACK. Delta_x = x_UP - x_DOWN cancels every feature that is the
same from both sides: seconds_left, |extension|, chop, spread, trade count,
volatility, reachability_touch, deep-book persistence, sigma. Those are
exactly the features that say "how much time and how much volatility is
left", which is most of what the barrier physics is. Under your form they
get weight zero by construction.

The coherent form that keeps them:

    logit(P_UP) = logit(P0_UP) + w . Delta_x + v . x_sym + b

where x_sym are side-symmetric features (identical for UP and DOWN) and
Delta_x are the side-relative differences. P_DOWN = 1 - P_UP still holds
exactly because there is one logit. This is a two-class softmax with a
shared-feature term, which is standard. Please adopt it.

THE SECOND CHANGE, forced by Section 7: the L0 term must carry a FITTED
slope, not a fixed coefficient of 1.

    logit(P_UP) = a * logit(P0_UP) + w . Delta_x + v . x_sym + b

Every Brownian-form prior assumes the current lead persists. Section 7
shows that on 2026-08-01 it mostly did not: with 100-60 s left the body
held only ~57 % of the time, and fair_p_up was confidently wrong through
the middle of the candle (Brier above 0.25 = worse than a coin). A fixed
offset forces L1 to undo that with the feature weights, which it can only
do approximately. A slope a < 1 shrinks an overconfident prior in one
parameter, prequentially, and a = 1 recovers your form exactly when the
prior is right. This is the cheapest single change with the largest
calibration effect I can point to in this data.

Feature policy for L1 v1: your 20-30 dimension predeclared set, agreed,
split into x_sym (time, |ext|, chop, sigma, reachability, deep persistence)
and Delta_x (aligned flow at 250 ms/1 s/5 s, persistence, transition, near
and deep book, OFI, replenishment, old-side aggression/futility, control
handoff, exhaustion, control transfer, fake, real, new-side effectiveness).
Challengers get the full 75.

Session/hour: telemetry only in v1. Conceded.

11. ABSOLUTE EDGE VS ROI
======================================================================

You are right and I was wrong. With a fixed dollar stake, expected dollars
= stake x E[R], and E[R] = P/P_BE - 1 is ROI. Admitting on absolute edge
alone would leave money on the table at low q for no reason.

Adopted: BOTH floors on a conservative probability.

    P_safe          = clamp(P_cal - buffer, .01, .99)
    prob_edge_safe  = P_safe - P_BE          >= delta_min
    roi_safe        = P_safe / P_BE - 1      >= roi_min

    buffer = clamp(max(ECE_prequential_global, |bucket_error(P_cal)|,
                       small_sample_floor), 0.02, 0.10)

The buffer is what makes ROI safe at low q. The attached policy sim has a
crude fixed buffer column so you can see its effect today (Section 14).
