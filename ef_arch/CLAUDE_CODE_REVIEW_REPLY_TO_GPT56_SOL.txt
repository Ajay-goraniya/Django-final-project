        architecture                  no-bias  c=.15   c=.25  worst
        EF-style contrarian (=A03)    +177.9   +33.3   -4.5   -4.5

    realistic bias assumption every contrarian design goes negative; the
    favourite-side learner does not move.

    claiming an architecture that structurally excludes it is wrong.

3.4 The constant-price synthetic PnL is optimistic in a specific, structural

7. BEST ARCHITECTURE IN YOUR VIEW
======================================================================

Working name: EF-DUAL (Dual-side, Unified, Adaptive, Liquidity-priced).

It is EF ARC with four structural changes and a different model ladder.

CHANGE 1 -- Two-sided candidate generation.
    Every eligible tick births up to two candidates: CONTINUATION (buy the
    side the candle is on) and REVERSAL (your contrarian EF). Same feature
    vector, same probability model, same economics layer. The market
    decides which is +EV at this price. Contrarian is kept because it is
    what earns in chop; continuation is added because it is what survived
    every pricing assumption and is the only design that was positive in
    trends. Section 13 shows why this alone is most of the bad-market
    protection.

CHANGE 2 -- Time and price are gradients, not gates.
    P(win | evidence, seconds_left) is the model's job. seconds_left and
    extension_sigma go in as continuous features and via a barrier-crossing
    prior (your ARCH I). The > $0.47 / late-fire losses disappear by
    construction when EV = P - 1.02*q is the admission variable: near-open
    candles have q ~ 0.50 and P ~ 0.50, so EV ~ -0.02 and they never fire.
    No "price > 0.50 reject" rule needed. This is your Section 15 taken
    seriously.

CHANGE 3 -- Model ladder, not model choice.
    L0: fixed barrier-crossing prior (no parameters to fit; your ARCH I).
    L1: regularised logistic on the causal feature vector, walk-forward,
        train-only standardisation, Platt/isotonic calibration when
        settled episodes >= 300 per regime bucket. This is what runs on
        day one and what my sweep used.
    L2: HistGB / ExtraTrees, promoted only when it beats L1 walk-forward
        over >= 5,000 episodes with the permutation and ablation tests
        passed. sklearn import failure -> fall back to L1, log loudly.
    Anchor+recent applies at whatever level is live, with windows in
    EPISODES (Section 10).

CHANGE 4 -- Exit and re-entry.
    After fire, keep evaluating. If calibrated P falls below the current
    bid for the held side minus a cost buffer, sell. If P rises while price
    falls, allow one scale-in up to the Kelly cap. Every loser today costs
    -1.00; this is where a meaningful fraction of that comes back.

Everything else in EF ARC stays: minimal safety gates, broad candidates,
counterfactual capture on every candidate, one observation per episode,
immediate lane + 250ms latch, regime as reliability modifier, no quota,
starvation diagnostic, PnL/100 as the objective.

10. LEARNING DESIGN
======================================================================

Training unit: one observation per (candle, direction, episode). Snapshot:
your Option B, the first decision-eligible snapshot, with Option D as a
bounded supplement -- at most 3 chronological snapshots at birth, +60s,
+120s, weights 0.5/0.3/0.2 summing to 1.0. Never the strongest-in-hindsight.
Rationale: B is the moment the system would have acted; D lets the model
see how the same episode looked as time ran out, which is the barrier
physics.

Label: settled contrarian/continuation outcome. Secondary label once quotes
exist: realised return per $1 at the snapshot's own VWAP.

Windows in EPISODES, not candles:
    recent model: last N = max(1000, 3 days) settled episodes
    retrain: every 100 settled episodes (~1 hour at current rates), not 24
             candles
    anchor: retrained offline, promoted manually, >= 10,000 episodes
Blend: start 0.7/0.3; move to reliability-weighted once each model has
>= 300 settled episodes of Brier history: w_recent = clamp(0.3 +
0.4*(brier_anchor - brier_recent)/brier_anchor, 0.15, 0.5).

Overreaction control: recent model has L2 = 0.05 (L1 logistic) or
max_depth <= 4 / min_leaf >= 25 (GBM); its authority is the blend weight
only; it cannot touch margins or gates. If recent Brier exceeds anchor
Brier by 20% over 300 episodes, w_recent -> 0.15 and a diagnostic fires.

One candle, bounded weight: the (candle, direction) key caps total training
weight at 1.0 regardless of how many ticks the websocket delivered. Build
36's ef_candidates already deduplicates this way; keep it.

Calibration: Platt on the blended logit, refit every 300 settled episodes,
train-only. Isotonic only when >= 2,000 episodes. Per-regime calibration
offset only when that regime has >= 300 episodes; otherwise global.
Report Brier, ECE (10 bins), and realised-vs-predicted by bucket every
retrain.

11. REAL ECONOMICS / PNL DESIGN
======================================================================

Exact formula, matching Build 36's ef_expected_edge and hypothetical_return
(PREDICT_FEE_RATE = 0.02):

    q      = executable full-stake VWAP for the side, from
             book.executable_vwap(side, stake).  NOT best ask.
    q'     = q * (1 + fee)                       all-in cost per share
    shares = stake / q'
    ret_win  = 1/q' - 1                          per $1 staked
    ret_loss = -1
    EV     = P * (1/q') - 1  =  P/q' - 1         per $1 staked
    edge   = P - q'                              per share (your form)
    break-even P = q'

Admission:
    EV <  margin_regime         -> no fire (default margin 0.06 = Build 36's
                                   EF_EV_MARGIN; do not lower it until
                                   calibration is proven)
    margin <= EV < 2*margin     -> latch lane
    EV >= 2*margin              -> immediate lane

Sizing (fractional Kelly on the binary):
    f* = (P - q') / (1 - q')
    f  = 0.25 * f*, capped at the staking layer's per-trade maximum
    At P=0.55, q'=0.306: f* = 0.352, f = 0.088 of bankroll.

Sensitivity you should internalise (at P=0.526, q=0.50):
    +1 point of P     -> +1.96% EV
    -1 cent of q      -> +2.11% EV
One cent of execution is worth one point of accuracy. Execution quality is
a first-class model input, not a slippage footnote.

Counterfactual capture on every candidate, fired or not: timestamp, side,
q, best_ask, shares, stake, quote_age, quote_reason, P, EV, regime, and at
settlement the realised return at q. Two tables: trades and counterfactuals.
This is what turns econ_examples from 0 into a dataset.

Where frequency comes from without firing garbage: the rejected pool. 477

Two-sided candidates roughly double the eligible pool for free.

Starvation stays diagnostic, but make its reasons operational:
    - eligible candidates/100 candles       (generator health)
    - share with quote available            (venue health)
    - share with EV > 0 before margin        (pricing health)
    - share admitted                        (margin health)
    - realised vs predicted P last 300      (calibration health)
If admitted/100 falls under 10 for 6 hours, page a human with those five
numbers. Do not auto-loosen anything.
