2. STRATEGY AS RUN (frozen, no per-day tuning)
======================================================================
  favourite at T = open + X   : TAPE PROXY: token whose last executed price before T is higher (tie / one side
                                untraded -> abstain: 29 of 8,238 decision rows).  REAL LADDERS: higher (bid+ask)/2, tie or
                                one-sided book on either token -> abstain (56 of 606).  NOT ask > 0.50.
  fill                        : TAPE PROXY: VWAP of the actual taker-BUY prints on that token from T(+delay) until their
                                notional covers the stake, max 15 s; no cover -> unexecutable, no trade.
                                REAL LADDERS: walk the favourite's ask ladder at the latest snapshot <= T(+delay).
  one trade per market, hold to settlement, outcome = Gamma outcomePrices (market's own Chainlink resolution).
  offsets 30/60/90/120/180/240 s on every date; 120 s is the primary. Nothing retuned.
  resolution wording check (your Section 14): ALL FIVE dates' markets carry the Chainlink TWAP wording
  ("time-weighted average price ... BTC/USD TWAP data stream"); Aug 1 carried the point end-vs-start wording.
