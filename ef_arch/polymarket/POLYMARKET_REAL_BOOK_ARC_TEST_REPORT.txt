4. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE GOAL
======================================================================

The user's goal is PnL with useful frequency that survives bad sessions.
Against real prediction-market prices on this day:

  a. The EF reversal thesis, as a BTC-microstructure signal, has no edge over
     the market price. Not "needs tuning" -- the price out-predicts the
     model 0.79 to 0.58, and the model adds nothing on top of the price.
  b. Buying the reversal side is structurally -EV here because the market
     overprices it. Every admission policy that keeps buying it loses; the
     ones that lose least are the ones that trade least.
  c. The favourite side carries a small, measurable positive edge with no
     signal at all, at retail size, before latency.

I am not recommending (c) as a strategy from one day. I am reporting that
it is the only positive number in the real-book test, and that it points
the same way my synthetic sweep pointed weeks ago: continuation survives,
reversal does not.
